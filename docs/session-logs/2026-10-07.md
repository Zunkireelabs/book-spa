## Focus: unblock staging deploy — wrap migration-249's RLS helper calls

Staging deploy's `migrate` step applied 246–253 fine, but the post-apply
assertion `scripts/check-rls-initplan.sh` failed: two `public.service_therapists`

recorded on staging (and prod), so it can't be edited in place — fix had to
