## Focus: dashboard revenue-vs-sales mismatch (Lazimpat branch, 2026-10-03)

User reported Admin Dashboard "TODAY" Net Revenue (NPR 68,588) not matching
their manual sum of booking amounts (NPR 77,787). Investigated live against
the production DB (Lazimpat branch, nuad-thai-spa org) rather than guessing
from code alone — first hypothesis (gift vouchers excluded via
`WALLET_MODES`) was wrong and corrected after checking actual rows; real
cause found by querying `payments`, `vouchers`/`voucher_payments`, and
`membership_transactions` directly.

**Root cause:** `getTodayInsights()` and `getDailySummary()`
(`src/services/api.js`) already folded voucher and product sale payments
into the Cash/Card/MobileBanking breakdown and `totalSales`/`netRevenue`
totals, but **membership top-ups and package sales were never folded in** —
only added as an unbucketed lump sum to `netRevenue`, and never added to
`totalSales` at all. Confirmed via a real row: today's NPR 25,000 membership
deposit was tendered via `MobileBanking` — real new cash — but the
dashboard's Total Sales figure and its Cash/Card/MobileBanking split both
missed it entirely. This is a straight undercount bug, not intentional
cash-basis exclusion (that policy only applies to `WALLET_MODES`
redemptions — paying *from* an existing membership/voucher balance — not to
*funding* one).

- Files modified (`src/services/api.js`):
  - `getWalletProductSalesForDate()` — now selects `payment_mode`
    (membership deposits) / `payment_method` (packages) and returns a
    `modeBreakdown` (cash/card/fonepay) alongside the existing totals.
  - `getDailySummary()` — merges `modeBreakdown` into `paymentBreakdown`, so
    the Net Revenue card's payment-mode split now includes membership/
    package cash.
  - `getTodayInsights()` — membership-deposit section (5) and package-sold
    section (6) now fold their amounts into `totalSales`/`modeTotals`, same
    pattern products (7b) and vouchers (2b) already used. Fixes the Total
    Sales panel being undercounted by the day's membership/package cash, not
    just a breakdown-labeling issue.
