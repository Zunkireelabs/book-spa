### Net Pay Formula
```
per_day          = monthly_salary ÷ actual_days_in_month
deduction        = per_day × (absent_days + 0.5 × half_days)   [Leave not deducted]
net_pay          = monthly_salary − deduction + service_commission + referral_commission
service_commission = Σ final_amount (Completed + paid, this therapist) × commission_rate%
referral_commission = Σ referral commissions on paid bookings where referred_by ≈ therapist.name
```
