STAFF ACCESS · Rules & constraints · P1 · new

Rules & constraints

Pure spec — no UI. What an engineer needs before touching the write path.
Two tables, no FK
Zenly keeps two separate tables today — therapists (the staff who actually deliver services, scheduled on the calendar) and users (login accounts). They have never been linked by any foreign key, and no staff member currently shares an email with any login — so the system can't automatically tell "this login" and "this staff member" are the same person. Name matching is a display-time heuristic, not a stored fact — of the 6 accounts whose name matches a staff record, 2 match more than one staff row; the "Link to staff" picker (panel 4) surfaces that ambiguity rather than silently guessing.

Granting access creates that link for the first time — it does not already exist in the data today. The six linked rows shown as EXAMPLE in panel 2's row-states breakdown are illustrative only, to show what a linked row will look like once this ships; the real linked count right now is zero.
The numbers (org-scoped)
Staff access is org-scoped — every figure below is Nuad Thai Spa's alone, not a cross-tenant total (staging holds five orgs; the other four are much smaller). Of the 16 accounts with no staff record, 6 share a name with one; the other 10 are owners, office admins, demo logins, and people whose staff row may exist under a different spelling.
Staff: 63 (61 active) Login accounts: 16 Already linked: 0 today (6 shown as examples) Service staff without login: 46 Support staff, login not usually needed: 15 Accounts with no staff record: 16
What "grant" writes
Creates the first link between a users row and a therapists row. Exact column (a direct FK vs. a join table) is not decided yet — see open questions. "Link to staff" is the inverse of "Give access" — it attaches an existing login to an existing staff record, for a login that already exists, rather than creating one.
Never moves or duplicates bookings, payments, or calendar history — those stay owned by the therapist id they already reference.

Every one of the 16 login accounts already has a 4-digit PIN set, which is why "Only PIN access" is viable as a login mode today, not a future dependency.
Home branch rule
Staff can always access their rostered home branch, even under "Selected branches" with the box unticked. This is a floor, not a toggle — it cannot be removed from the UI.
