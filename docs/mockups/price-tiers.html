PRICE TIERS · Rules & open questions · P1 · new

Rules & open questions

Pure spec — the decided model, the rejected alternative, and what's still unanswered.
Decided model
Tier pricing is a premium for naming a specific staff member — the value is letting a customer choose who serves them, and pay for that choice.
"Any available" → the service's base price (services.price_npr).
Naming someone → that person's tier price.
Rejected alternativenot this
"Price follows the person" — whoever actually performs the service sets the price, regardless of what was requested or who was originally assigned. The team hasn't seen the two side by side yet; kept here as a labelled contrast, not given equal billing in the main flow.
Three open consequence cases
1. Reassignment after booking. Customer books "Any available" at base; a Senior is assigned later.
proposal Price locks at booking time; reassignment never changes it. Already consistent with existing behavior — service-detail/index.jsx already warns that price/duration changes "only affect future bookings; historical bookings are protected by snapshot fields." The lock this proposes isn't new machinery, it's the same snapshot discipline already in place.
2. Requested staff doesn't deliver. Customer paid the Senior premium; a Junior covers.
proposal Re-price down and refund the difference — collides with the immutable-payments rule in CLAUDE.md (RESTRICT on UPDATE/DELETE), so this is a refund record, not an edit to the original payment.
3. Multi-therapist bookings. booking_therapists is a junction; combos carry per-item staff in booking_combo_items.
proposal Per-line pricing — each line priced at that line's staff member's tier, and whether the line was requested or auto-assigned decides base vs. tier.
Scale problem — resolved
196 services × 4+ tiers is not a usable grid as-is — no category grouping, no search, no bulk-set. But Timely's own tier pricing (their Settings/ServiceEdit page) never shows that matrix: tier prices are set per service, on that service's own edit page, a handful of rows at a time. You're already there when you care about that service's price. The grid never needs to scale past one row of tiers, because it was never the UI — see Service price section above.

Price range across those 196 services today: NPR 150 – NPR 100,000 (the top end is membership packages, not treatments).
Branch dimension
Price does not vary by branch today — branch_service_links has no price column. Tiers are the first dimension of price variation. If per-branch pricing is ever wanted too, service × tier × branch is a different schema than service × tier — decide the shape now, not after tiers ship.
Tiers vs. therapists.positionopen question
position is free text — 23 distinct values across 82 people in staging, 13 blank, with near-duplicates like "Brow And Lash Artist" / "Brow & Lash Artist" (same two values flagged in the staff-access board). It's the closest thing to a tier today, and it's unusable as one in its current state. Open question: do tiers replace position, or sit beside it? Having both invites contradiction — a "Junior" tier assignment next to a "Senior Therapist" position label on the same person.
Junior priced below baseopen question
The pricing grid above puts Junior at NPR 3,800 against a NPR 4,200 base for the Hot Oil Massage. Under the decided model, naming the Junior costs less than "Any available" — which can also be assigned that same Junior. Requesting a specific person ends up cheaper than not caring, the inverse of what a "pay to choose" feature is supposed to incentivize. Open question: should tier prices be allowed below base at all, and if so, what stops "Any available" from being the worst-value option on the menu?

Same family of problem: Timely labels the base row "Unassigned" — the price for staff on no tier — not "Any available." The two usually land on the same number, but not always: if a customer requests an untiered person by name, is that base (nobody's tier applies) or a request-premium with no tier to price it from? We haven't picked.
Price modes (Fixed / Variable / From)open question
Timely's Price field is a "Fixed price" dropdown, implying other modes exist. Tier pricing as specced here assumes fixed pricing throughout — nothing on this board proposes variable or "from" pricing. Open question: does Zenly need non-fixed price modes at all, or is tiered-fixed enough? Not building toward modes we have no requirement for.
