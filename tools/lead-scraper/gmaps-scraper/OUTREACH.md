# Outreach Plan

## Channel (default: WhatsApp first, call second)
- Mobile number: WhatsApp message, then a call 2 days later if there's no reply.
- Landline only: call during quiet hours (11am–1pm, when salons are slow).
- SMS is a later option. See the SMS/WhatsApp note in project memory. It is available on request, not as a default.

## Rules
- Manual and personal at first. About 20–30 leads a day, not blasts.
- Name the business and mention something real (area, rating, a service they offer).
- One clear ask: a 10-minute demo or a free trial.
- If someone says no, mark `no` and never contact them again.

## First-message template (edit before use)
> Namaste {owner/name}, I came across {business} in {area}, and your {rating}★ reviews are great.
> We built Zennly, booking and client management software for {salons/spas/…} in Nepal. It covers online booking, reminders, staff schedules and payments in one place.
> Can I show you a quick 10-minute demo? {solution page link}

## Follow-up cadence
| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| 0 | First WhatsApp message |
| 2 | Call if there's no reply |
| 5 | One short nudge with a benefit (e.g. fewer no-shows) |
| 12 | Final message, then close as `no` |

## Tracking
Update `status`, `contacted_on` and `notes` in the CSV (or a Google Sheet) after each touch.
Weekly numbers: contacted, replied, demos, customers. Use these to see which categories and areas convert best.
